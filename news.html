SATURDAY, 21 NOVEMBER 2026
Missiles over Bahrain, a cartel kingpin captured, and climate talks in overtime.
LEAD STORY  ·  MIDDLE EAST

IRAN STRIKES BAHRAIN

Iran’s Revolutionary Guards say they fired missiles and drones at US military positions in Bahrain on Saturday night, naming the Al-Riffa and Awali bases.

Bahrain says an attack hit a civilian industrial facility, setting it on fire, with no injuries; its air defences intercepted attacks. It is the fourth Iranian attack on Bahrain in nine days. Bahrain hosts the US Navy’s Fifth Fleet.

AMERICAS

Mexico Captures “El Chapo Isidro”

Special forces seized Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, one of the FBI’s 10 most-wanted fugitives, in Guasave, Sinaloa, on Friday night. 11 people were killed in the raid and the violence that followed.

UKRAINE

Kryvyi Rih Double-Tap Strike

Russian missiles hit President Zelenskyy’s hometown on Friday night, killing a 7-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman and wounding 60 people, six of them children. A second wave struck rescuers.

EUROPE

Russian Missiles Land in Poland

Two Russian missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland on Friday during military exercises. NATO chief Mark Rutte, in Warsaw: “NATO will defend every inch of Polish territory.”

CLIMATE

COP31 Talks in Overtime

The UN climate summit in Antalya ran past its Friday close with no deal. The draft text omits fossil fuels entirely; the draft admits the 1.5°C goal has been missed. Nearly 200 countries are negotiating.

MARKETS

Markets in Correction

US stocks slid again as the AI and chip sell-off deepened. The Nasdaq erased all its 2026 gains; the S&P 500 and Dow fell into correction territory after a rocky debut for Anthropic.

DIPLOMACY

Trump Tells Europe: Defund Ukraine

After his three-way summit with Putin and Xi in Shenzhen, President Trump told European leaders to withhold funding from Ukraine. Zelenskyy called Putin “a killer”.

WORLD  ·  PERU QUAKE

Magnitude 6.7 Shakes Peru

A 6.7 earthquake shook Peru’s central Andes on Friday evening, felt in Lima; no casualties reported.

SCIENCE  ·  DENGUE BREAKTHROUGH

Mosquitoes Cut Dengue by 69%

A four-year trial in Belo Horizonte found Wolbachia mosquitoes cut dengue and chikungunya by about 69% — 5,700 children studied.
